INDIAN VEHICLES · INTERNATIONAL WORKSHOPS

Indian vehicle support.
Connected worldwide.

Vehicles designed, manufactured or assembled in India operate across South Asia, Africa, the Middle East, Latin America and other markets. This hub helps vehicle owners, mechanics, auto electricians and workshops find the right route for ECU file enquiries, airbag module support, fault-code diagnosis, wiring information and technical discussion. Every request is checked against the exact vehicle and module details before support is confirmed.

Passenger cars and SUVs

Vehicle families to identify accurately include:

  • Maruti Suzuki
  • Tata Motors
  • Mahindra
  • Force Motors

Record model, generation, year, engine, transmission, market variant and ECU hardware/software IDs. A model name alone is not enough to establish file compatibility.

Trucks, buses and commercial vehicles

Workshop enquiries may involve Tata Motors Commercial Vehicles, Ashok Leyland, Eicher / VE Commercial Vehicles, SML Mahindra, Force Motors, and other India-built commercial platforms.

Include chassis or platform family where appropriate, engine and emissions system, control-module numbers, diagnostic codes and read-tool details. Do not publish customer or vehicle identifiers in public forum posts.

Tractors and agricultural equipment

Include Mahindra and Swaraj, TAFE / Massey Ferguson, Sonalika, Escorts Kubota, Eicher, VST and other tractor or farm-equipment families where the relevant electronic module is supported.

State tractor model, engine, year, controller label, part number and the exact fault or service requirement.

Two-wheelers and three-wheelers

Relevant India-based manufacturer families include Hero MotoCorp, TVS, Bajaj, Royal Enfield, Atul Auto and Piaggio Vehicles, subject to the type of electronic module and service supported.

Share the exact model and variant. Motorcycle, scooter, auto-rickshaw and passenger-car systems are not interchangeable.

International markets to prioritise

Official Indian export and manufacturer market information points to strong relevance across these regions. The list is a market-priority guide, not a claim that every Indian brand or model is sold in every country.

  • South Asia: Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Bhutan.
  • Africa: South Africa, Kenya, Ghana, Tanzania, Morocco, Tunisia, Mozambique and other African export markets.
  • Middle East: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman.
  • Latin America: Mexico, Chile, Peru, Brazil, Argentina and neighbouring markets.
  • Asia-Pacific: Japan, Indonesia, Australia, New Zealand and nearby markets.

Priority is guided by official export information: India’s 2024–25 passenger-vehicle destinations included South Africa, Saudi Arabia, Mexico, Japan, UAE and Chile; commercial-vehicle destinations included Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Indonesia, UAE, Bangladesh, Ghana, Morocco, Nepal and Tanzania. Individual manufacturer line-ups differ by country.

ECU, DTC and file support

For a useful review, provide vehicle make/model/year, engine and fuel type, ECU maker and hardware number, software/calibration ID, read method/tool, original file type and size, fault codes and a clear description of the issue.

Compatibility, legal requirements and serviceability are reviewed before accepting work. Do not assume a file from another country or variant is interchangeable.

Airbag, wiring and workshop help

For SRS or airbag enquiries, provide the exact module part number and vehicle variant. Safety-critical work must follow manufacturer procedures and local regulations; bypassing or disabling safety protections is not supported.

Use the public forum for general, non-sensitive diagnostic questions. Keep VINs, registration numbers, names, contact details and private files out of public posts.

Brand names are used only to help identify vehicle families; no manufacturer endorsement or affiliation is implied. Porsche is a German manufacturer, not an Indian-origin vehicle brand. Other international makes may be considered separately only when the exact vehicle and module are within the service capability. Availability is not universal and must be confirmed for each request.
