BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Help us identify
the correct ECU file.

Accurate vehicle and ECU identification helps avoid mismatched files. Send the original readout and the details below so the request can be checked before any work is accepted.

01 / VEHICLE DETAILS

Identify the vehicle

Include the vehicle brand, model, model year, engine capacity or engine code, fuel type and transmission where known. Mention the exact warning, fault symptoms and any diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs).

02 / ECU IDENTIFICATION

Share ECU information

Provide the ECU manufacturer and model, plus the hardware number, software number and calibration identification shown by your diagnostic or reading tool. A clear label or identification-screen photo can help when available.

03 / ORIGINAL READOUT

Keep the original file

Upload the unmodified original readout from your compatible tool. BIN, ORI, MOD or HEX files may be reviewed where supported. Keep a separate backup and do not rename or alter file contents to make them appear compatible.

COMPATIBILITY REVIEW

Common questions

Submitting a request starts a review; it does not guarantee that a particular vehicle, ECU version or file operation can be supported.

Can workshops outside India submit a request?

Yes. International workshops can submit requests through the same secure form. Availability is reviewed case by case using the vehicle, ECU hardware/software identifiers, original readout quality, technical feasibility and applicable local requirements. Submission does not guarantee compatibility or acceptance.

Are all vehicle brands and ECU versions supported?

No. Support depends on the exact vehicle, ECU hardware/software identifiers, original readout quality, available technical information and applicable requirements. Compatibility is checked case by case.

What happens after I upload a file?

The submitted details and original readout are reviewed first. If the combination is compatible and the requested work is appropriate, the next steps are shared through your request. Additional information may be requested if identification is incomplete.

Can I send a modified file or skip ECU details?

Please send the original readout and provide all available identification details. A modified or incomplete file can prevent reliable verification and may delay or prevent the review.

Are emissions and vehicle-security systems handled?

Requests are assessed only for work that is authorised and consistent with applicable laws and required vehicle safety and emissions controls. Vehicle-security related work requires appropriate ownership authorisation.
