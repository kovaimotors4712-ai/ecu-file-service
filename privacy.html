YOUR DATA & FILES

Privacy Policy.

This page explains how ECU File Service India handles information when you browse the website, create an account, submit a request, make a payment or use the workshop community.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the feature you use, we may process account and sign-in details, vehicle and ECU information, service-request messages, files you upload, support communications and information needed to fulfil an order.

2. Uploaded files and private storage

Original and processed service files are intended for private, access-controlled storage. Access is restricted by authentication, order ownership and server-side authorization. Do not upload files or personal information unless you are authorised to provide them.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed by the payment provider shown at checkout. The website verifies payment results server-side. Never send card details, bank credentials, UPI PINs or one-time passwords in a support message.

4. Community content

Forum topics and public chat messages are intended to be visible to community users. Do not post customer names, contact details, vehicle identification numbers, private files or other confidential information in public discussions.

5. How information is used

  • To authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • To review requests, process verified payments and deliver purchased services.
  • To provide support, security checks and service notifications.
  • To operate and improve the website and prevent abuse.

6. Service providers and retention

Hosting, database, storage, email and payment providers may process information as needed to operate the service. Information is retained for operational, security, dispute-resolution and legal needs, then deleted or de-identified when appropriate and feasible.

7. Security and your choices

We use access controls and private storage, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Keep your password confidential, sign out on shared devices and contact the team through your signed-in account if you need help with a request or account-data issue.

8. Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website and its features change. The current version is published on this page.

For service questions or privacy-related requests, sign in and use the private request/support feature so the message is handled privately. Do not include sensitive account credentials in your message.
